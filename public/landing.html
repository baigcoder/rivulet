Playback Architecture

A real native window, not a browser video tag.

Desktop embeds real mpv (via X11/Win32/macOS libmpv). Android embeds libVLC with native hardware decoders. Both answer the same command protocol with zero browser codec restrictions.

Native Player Window

Chromium's browser <video> tag strips Dolby (AC-3, E-AC-3, TrueHD) and DTS codecs by default. Rivulet's native backends decode every audio format and container natively on your device.

  • Seek-preview thumbnails warmed while hovering progress
  • Unified track menu for file subs, audio tracks, and OpenSubtitles
  • Audio-driven auto-sync aligns subtitles via silence mapping

Hardware Codec Support

Whether playing high-bitrate 4K REMUXes or anime encodes, Rivulet leverages your device's GPU decoders with zero transcode overhead.

  • HEVC / H.265 & AV1 Video Decoding
  • 10-bit HDR & Dolby Vision passthrough
  • E-AC-3 (Dolby Digital Plus), DTS-HD & TrueHD Audio
  • MKV, MP4, TS & Partial Download file playback

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know

Does Rivulet provide or bundle any content?
No. Rivulet hosts no content, indexes no content, and ships with no sources pre-configured. It is a general-purpose local media player and BitTorrent client for links and files you choose to add.
Do I need an account or sign-in?
No. There are zero accounts, sign-ins, or cloud trackers. Your watch progress, favorites, and history live 100% on your local device.
Does Rivulet work on Android TV with a remote control?
Yes! Rivulet features a dedicated 10-foot TV UI with full D-Pad remote navigation, focus rings, and Android TV leanback launcher support.
Why does desktop use mpv instead of a browser video tag?
Browser video tags drop support for Dolby AC-3, E-AC-3, DTS, HEVC, and 10-bit HDR on many operating systems. Embedding real mpv allows native hardware decoding for every container and codec out of the box.
How does playback start on the first bytes of a torrent?
The in-process librqbit BitTorrent engine prioritizes header and early video pieces first, feeding stream buffers directly into mpv or libVLC as soon as the initial bytes arrive.
