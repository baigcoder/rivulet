Dual-Engine Architecture

Two streaming engines. One unified player.

Rivulet seamlessly handles BitTorrent sequential P2P downloading and zero-cache HTTP/HLS direct playback inside the exact same native player window.

Mode 1 · BitTorrent Swarm librqbit Rust Engine

Sequential Torrent Streaming

Stream high-bitrate torrents the moment you add a magnet link or .torrent file. The embedded Rust BitTorrent engine prioritizes early video headers and sequential chunks directly into the player window.

  • First-byte instant playback without waiting for full download
  • Magnet link & .torrent file drag-and-drop support
  • Granular file selection inside multi-file torrent archives
  • Configurable seeding limits & upload/download speed caps
  • Disk budget auto-eviction clears oldest watched torrents
💡 Best for: High quality 4K REMUXes, complete series packs, & P2P archives.
Mode 2 · Direct HTTP & HLS Zero Disk Overhead

Instant Direct Stream Play

Zero waiting, zero disk storage, zero P2P connection overhead. Stream raw HTTP, HTTPS, and HLS (.m3u8) video URLs directly into native GPU hardware decoders.

  • Sub-second stream startup without local disk writes
  • HTTP/HTTPS, HLS (.m3u8), and TS stream protocol support
  • Live stream automatic reconnects & latency recovery
  • Custom HTTP headers (User-Agent, Referer, Authorization)
  • Zero P2P upload bandwidth consumption
⚡ Best for: Fast playback, limited disk space, & Live TV streams.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know

Does Rivulet provide or bundle any content?
No. Rivulet hosts no content, indexes no content, and ships with no sources pre-configured. It is a general-purpose local media player and BitTorrent client for links and files you choose to add.
Do I need an account or sign-in?
No. There are zero accounts, sign-ins, or cloud trackers. Your watch progress, favorites, and history live 100% on your local device.
Does Rivulet work on Android TV with a remote control?
Yes! Rivulet features a dedicated 10-foot TV UI with full D-Pad remote navigation, focus rings, and Android TV leanback launcher support.
Why does desktop use mpv instead of a browser video tag?
Browser video tags drop support for Dolby AC-3, E-AC-3, DTS, HEVC, and 10-bit HDR on many operating systems. Embedding real mpv allows native hardware decoding for every container and codec out of the box.
How does playback start on the first bytes of a torrent?
The in-process librqbit BitTorrent engine prioritizes header and early video pieces first, feeding stream buffers directly into mpv or libVLC as soon as the initial bytes arrive.
