two ways to press play

Every release your sources return is one of two kinds, and Rivulet labels it before you choose. Either way, Play is one press.

instant from your cloud

When a source answers with a link from a cloud (debrid) service you subscribe to, the film streams straight from it. Nothing is downloaded and nothing waits for peers.

good to know

Does Rivulet come with any content or sources?

No. Rivulet hosts nothing, indexes nothing and ships with no sources. It searches only the servers you add yourself, and works as a player and torrent client with none at all.

What does "Instant (cloud)" mean?

Some sources answer with direct links from a cloud (debrid) service you subscribe to, rather than torrents. Rivulet tells the two apart when you add the source: cloud links play at once with nothing downloaded, torrents play through the built-in engine.

My source has no cloud service. Can I still press Play?

Yes. Play while downloading starts in seconds while the file lands on the drive you choose. If you'd rather keep nothing, stream from a temporary cache that is deleted when you leave the player.

Do I need an account?

No. There's no Rivulet account and no Rivulet server. Your progress, favourites and history stay on your device, and a backup file moves them to another one.

Does it work on Android TV with a remote?

Yes. One APK for phones, tablets and TVs. Every screen works with up, down, left, right, OK and back, with clear focus rings and a launcher tile on Android TV.

What is Premium TV?

A place to connect the IPTV service you already have, with an Xtream Codes login or an M3U playlist link. Rivulet doesn't sell or provide IPTV, and your login stays on your device.
