The player

A real player, not a browser video tag.

On Windows, Linux and macOS Rivulet embeds mpv; on Android it plays through libVLC. Both carry their own decoders, so what a browser drops just plays.

4KHDR 10-bitHEVCAV1H.264E-AC-3DTSTrueHDMKVMP4
  • Starts on the first bytes. A torrent plays while the rest downloads, and a finished one replays offline.
  • Fails over quietly. A dead link moves to the next server; a service's error clip is caught, never played.
  • Subtitles that fit. Files from the release and OpenSubtitles in one menu, auto-synced to the audio on desktop.
  • Seek preview and resume. Frames as you scrub, and every episode picks up where you left it.

Free TV

Public channels from around the world, numbered like a set and grouped by country and category. No account needed.

  • Dead channels hidden — every channel is checked in the background, and the header says how many are out.

Questions

Good to know.

Does Rivulet come with any content or sources?
No. Rivulet hosts nothing, indexes nothing and ships with no sources. It searches only the servers you add yourself, and it works as a general player and torrent client with none at all.
What does "Instant (cloud)" mean?
Some sources answer with direct links from a cloud (debrid) service you subscribe to, rather than torrents. Rivulet tells the two apart when you add the source: cloud links play at once with nothing downloaded, torrents play through the built-in engine.
My source has no cloud service. Can I still press Play?
Yes. Rivulet says so when you add it and suggests Play while downloading: playback starts in seconds while the file lands on the drive you choose. If you'd rather keep nothing, stream from a temporary cache that is deleted when you leave the player.
What happens when my cloud service is down?
The title page says so before you press Play, quoting what the service answered, and the player offers to play the same title from the torrent instead. Rivulet never plays a service's "unavailable" clip as your film.
Do I need an account?
No. There's no Rivulet account and no Rivulet server. Your progress, favourites and history stay on your device, and a backup file moves them to another one.
Does it work on Android TV with a remote?
Yes. Every screen works with up, down, left, right, OK and back, with clear focus rings and a launcher tile on Android TV.
Why a real player instead of a browser video tag?
Browser video drops Dolby, DTS, HEVC and 10-bit on many systems. mpv on desktop and libVLC on Android carry their own decoders, so those files simply play.
What is Premium TV?
A place to connect the IPTV service you already have, using an Xtream Codes login or an M3U playlist link. Rivulet doesn't sell or provide IPTV; your login stays on your device.
