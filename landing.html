A cloud service skips the wait

If a source you add answers with direct links from a cloud (debrid) service you pay for, Play starts in a second: no engine, no disk.

Play says what it will do

Each source is labelled Instant or Torrents when you add it. A torrent-only title asks first: download while playing, or stream from a cache deleted when you leave.

A service outage is said in words

When your cloud service is down, the title page says so before you press Play, with the torrent one tap away. Its error clip never plays as your film.

free tv

Thousands of public channels from around the world, numbered like a set and grouped by country and category. No account needed.

  • Every channel is checked as you open it; dead ones are marked, then hidden.
  • A guide from your own XMLTV links, with what's on now and how long is left.
  • Channel up and down, number keys, and 0 for the last channel.

premium tv

The IPTV service you already pay for, through an Xtream Codes login or an M3U link. Live channels, films and series side by side.

  • Catch-up on channels your provider keeps an archive for.
  • Several accounts, switched in a press; your login stays on this device.
  • A reminder a minute before a programme starts.

yours, not ours

good to know

Sources

No. Rivulet hosts nothing, indexes nothing and ships with no sources. It searches only the servers you add yourself, and works as a player and torrent client with none at all.

Cloud

Some sources answer with direct links from a cloud (debrid) service you subscribe to, rather than torrents. Rivulet tells the two apart when you add the source: cloud links play at once with nothing downloaded, torrents play through the built-in engine.

Torrents

Yes. Play while downloading starts in seconds while the file lands on the drive you choose. If you'd rather keep nothing, stream from a temporary cache that is deleted when you leave the player.

Account

No. There's no Rivulet account and no Rivulet server. Your progress, favourites and history stay on your device, and a backup file moves them to another one.

Remote

Yes. One APK for phones, tablets and TVs. Every screen works with up, down, left, right, OK and back, with clear focus rings and a launcher tile on Android TV.

Premium TV

A place to connect the IPTV service you already have, with an Xtream Codes login or an M3U playlist link. Rivulet doesn't sell or provide IPTV, and your login stays on your device.
